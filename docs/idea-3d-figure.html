Karmi — how it works, in 3D

The same three scenes the demo video shows: the approved house builds itself, reality lands on top, and the difference is highlighted.

The naksa becomes structured data (walls, doors, windows, rooms), and the renderer extrudes it — no AI in this step, just the drawing made visible.

Photos and hand-entered measurements become an "as-built" model — every value carries its confidence and its source.

In the real app this is an animation: the plan extrudes upward, the as-built overlays it, and you can orbit the model. Every element is clickable, showing expected vs observed with confidence.

Why 3D: a homeowner who cannot read a plan can still see a narrower window when the approved outline is ghosted next to the built one. The visual is the explanation — the Nepali checklist underneath tells them what to do about it.
